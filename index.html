A little about me

Creative heart.
Curious mind.

My name is Pastah Demih. I am a Ugandan and I am passionate about songwriting, music composition and creating digital content.

I have completed certificate-level studies in music and electrical work, and I am continuing to study and improve in both fields. I also sing in my church choir and help train the choir, using music to serve, learn and grow with others.

My goal is to keep developing my skills, share meaningful creative work, collaborate with others and build a future that brings together music and technical knowledge.
